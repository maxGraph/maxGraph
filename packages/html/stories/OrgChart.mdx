# OrgChart

This example demonstrates using automatic layouts, fit to page zoom and poster print (across multiple pages).

The graph is a tree, laid out automatically with a `CompactTreeLayout` each time a vertex is added or removed.

## What you can do

- **Pan** the graph by dragging the background with the left mouse button.
- **Navigate** with the outline displayed in the top right corner, which shows the whole graph and the visible area.
- **Add a child** to a vertex with its `+` overlay icon.
- **Delete a vertex** and its whole subtree with its close overlay icon. The root vertex cannot be deleted.
- **Edit a label** by double-clicking a vertex, or from the popup menu. See the Introduction page for how to validate or cancel the change. The label of the root vertex cannot be changed.
- **Zoom** with the toolbar. When zooming in, the labels display more details, without changing the size of the vertices.
- **Print** the graph on a single page, or as a poster spanning several pages, from the toolbar.
- Open the **popup menu** with a right click: on a vertex for the actions related to it, or on the background to fit, reset the zoom and print.
